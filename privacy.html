Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data the Athlete Circle app ("Athlete Circle", "we", "us") collects, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. Athlete Circle is operated by Ala Khattat, an individual developer. You can contact us at sportsync26@gmail.com.

1. What we collect

DataDetails
AccountYour email address and password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider).
ProfileName, username, profile photo, bio, city, and whether you are an athlete, a professional or both.
SportsYour sports, levels, goals and partner preferences (for example intensity or weight class).
LocationIf you allow it, your approximate and exact location. Your exact coordinates are stored privately and are never shown to anyone. Other members only ever see an approximate distance, rounded to bands of about 1 km.
PhotosPhotos you choose to upload: profile photo, post photos, chat photos, gear listing photos, service photos and verification documents. We only access photos you pick; the app does not browse your library.
MessagesMessages and photos you send in conversations.
CommunityPosts, comments and likes.
BookingsBooking requests, their status, dates, notes, and reviews and star ratings.
Crew & partnersPartner requests, crew members and your "free to train" status.
Gear BoardListings you post (title, description, condition, price shown to others, photos).
SafetyReports you make or receive, people you block, and moderation outcomes.
TechnicalLogs our service providers keep to run and secure the service (for example IP address, device and request information).

2. Why we use it

  • Matching: to suggest training partners and professionals by sport, level, preferences and distance.
  • Messaging and community: to deliver your messages, posts and comments.
  • Bookings: to let you request, accept, cancel and review sessions with professionals.
  • Safety and moderation: to screen certain photos for unsafe content, handle reports, enforce our Terms, and keep members safe.
  • Operating the service: authentication, storage, troubleshooting, security and app updates.

We do not show ads, we do not sell your data, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

3. Legal bases (EEA/UK)

  • Contract: to provide the features you sign up for.
  • Legitimate interests: safety, moderation, fraud prevention and keeping the service secure.
  • Consent: for device location and for photos you choose to share. You can withdraw consent at any time in your device settings.

4. Who we share it with

Other members see what you choose to make public: your profile, approximate distance, posts, gear listings and services. Messages are visible only to the people in the conversation (and to our moderators if a message is reported).

We use these service providers (processors) to run Athlete Circle:

  • Supabase, Inc. and its infrastructure providers: hosting, database, authentication and file storage.
  • Google Cloud Vision (Google LLC): automated screening of gear and service photos for unsafe content.
  • Expo / EAS (650 Industries, Inc.): building the app and delivering app updates.

We may also disclose data if required by law or to protect the safety of members.

5. Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete content (a post, a comment, a listing) it is removed. When you delete your account, your data is deleted as described below.

6. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app (Profile → Delete account) or by emailing sportsync26@gmail.com from your account address. Deletion is immediate and permanent. We delete your profile, location, photos, posts, comments, messages, crew, gear listings, services and verification documents. Conversations you were part of are removed for everyone in them. Upcoming bookings are cancelled and the other person is notified. We keep, without your name or any way to identify you, the outcome of reports you made or received, and star ratings from completed sessions. Backups are overwritten within 7 days.

More details: Delete your account.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to access, correct, delete or export (portability) your data, and object to or restrict certain processing. You can edit most of your data directly in the app. To exercise any right, email sportsync26@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection supervisory authority.

8. Children

Athlete Circle is not intended for anyone under 16, and you must be at least 16 to create an account. If you believe a younger person has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and protected by access rules so members can only read what they are allowed to. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
