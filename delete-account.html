Delete your Athlete Circle account

This page explains how to delete your account in the Athlete Circle app (by Ala Khattat) and what happens to your data.

Option 1: in the app (immediate)

  1. Open Athlete Circle and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  4. Read the summary, enter your password and tap Permanently delete my account.

Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out.

Option 2: by email

If you can't use the app, email sportsync26@gmail.com from the email address of your account with the subject "Delete my account". We'll delete it within 7 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details
  • Your profile, location, sports and preferences
  • All photos you uploaded (profile, posts, chats, gear, services) and verification documents
  • Your posts, comments and likes
  • Your messages and conversations
  • Your crew, partner requests and training status
  • Your gear listings and, for professionals, your services and availability

Conversations you were part of are removed for everyone in them. Upcoming bookings are cancelled and the other person is notified.

What is kept, without identifying you

  • The outcome of reports you made or received (with your name and content removed), so moderation records stay intact.
  • Star ratings from completed sessions (without your name or written review), so professionals' ratings stay fair.

Backups are overwritten within 7 days, after which nothing remains.
