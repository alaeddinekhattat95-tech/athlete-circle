FAQ

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen tap Forgot password?, enter your email and open the link we send you on your phone. You'll be able to choose a new password in the app.

How do I report or block someone?

Open their profile, a post, a comment, a message or a gear listing and use Report or Block. Blocked people can't contact you and you won't see their content. You can manage blocked people in Profile → Blocked users.

How do I delete my account?

See Delete your account below.

How does professional verification work?

Professionals can submit documents (for example certificates) from their service settings. We review them and, if they check out, add a verification badge. A badge reflects the documents reviewed at that time; it isn't a guarantee.

Who can see my location?

Nobody sees your exact location. Other members only see an approximate distance, rounded to about 1 km.

Delete your account

In the app (immediate):

  1. Open Athlete Circle and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  4. Read the summary, enter your password and tap Permanently delete my account.

Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out. This can't be undone.

If you can't open the app: email sportsync26@gmail.com from the email address of your account with the subject "Delete my account". We'll delete it within 7 days and confirm by email.

We delete your profile, location, photos, posts, comments, messages, crew, gear listings, services and verification documents. Conversations you were part of are removed for everyone in them, and upcoming bookings are cancelled with the other person notified. We keep, without your name, the outcome of reports and star ratings from completed sessions. Full details: what is deleted and kept.

Safety tips for meeting training partners

  • Chat in the app first and meet in a public place, such as a gym, club or park.
  • Tell a friend where you're going and when you expect to be back.
  • Agree on intensity and rules before sparring or rolling, and tap early.
  • Bring your own protective gear and check borrowed or bought gear before use.
  • Never send money in advance to someone you haven't met.
  • If anything feels wrong, leave, then report and block.
